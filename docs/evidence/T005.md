## Design coverage vs task requirements

- Phone + desktop: single-column/bottom-tab under 720px, top nav and grids
  above; e2e checks horizontal overflow on all 8 screens in both projects.
- Keyboard: skip link retained; radios/inputs/buttons are native controls;
  review rates with keys 1–4 and Space/Enter reveals; focus moves to the Next
  control after feedback; `:focus-visible` styling extended to buttons/inputs.
- Accessible labels: nav aria-labels, `aria-current`, table captions,
  `aria-busy` loaders, `role=status` feedback/progress/save notices,
  `role=alert` errors, fieldset legends, labeled suspend checkboxes.
- Beyond color: every status pairs a text label with a distinct symbol;
  mastery/schedule/outcome vocabularies never share labels.
- States: each screen exposes a `?state=` switcher (normal/loading/empty/
  error/forbidden/unavailable/evaluating as applicable). Forbidden is
  demonstrated as unenrolled-track on the map; guard-level denial stays with
  `/app`.
- Signal separation: grammar mastery (evidence), flashcard scheduling, and
  session outcomes are separate labeled panels; copy states the separation.
- Prerequisites: shown as "Recommended first"/"Not yet covered" hints with
  anchor links; every lesson remains openable.
- Anki: optional user-initiated one-way export with explicit no-due-dates/
  no-history disclosure; no sync or connection settings exist.

  resets on full reload. Intervals, mastery labels, and counts are
  illustrative, not tuned behavior.

  cutoff, T017/T018 mastery thresholds, T034 deck editing/Cloze scope, T033
  scheduler fuzz, multi-device resume, assessment/mixed-review modes.
- The prototype e2e spec asserts structure/roles/states and avoids pinning
  website wording beyond accessible names used by the checks.
- `?state=` variants are a review convenience, not a production error-handling
  contract.
